JDI Statement on the Netanyahu Government's Sanctions Against Haaretz

We are former editors of the South African newspaper the Weekly Mail, writing on behalf of our colleagues in the Jewish Democratic Initiative, an organisation which campaigns for free speech, human rights, an end to the occupation and a just and equitable peace in Israel-Palestine.

We write to express our solidarity with Haaretz at this difficult time.

The Netanyahu government's sanctions against Haaretz have brought back vivid memories of our own newspaper's struggles against the apartheid government some four decades ago. Indeed, while the Israeli government is enraged by any suggestion of apartheid-like behaviour, it continues to act from the same playbook.

Haaretz is one of Israel's oldest and most distinguished newspapers. Its journalism, including its coverage of the occupation and its human cost, represents precisely the kind of independent, courageous reporting that a healthy democracy requires. Sanctioning a newspaper for its editorial positions is an attack on press freedom and democratic norms — wherever it occurs.

We stand in full solidarity with the journalists and editors of Haaretz. A free press is not a luxury; it is a cornerstone of democracy. Those who attack it — whether in South Africa under apartheid or in Israel today — are on the wrong side of history.

Statement by the Jewish Democratic Initiative, December 2024. Also published on Haaretz and covered by News24.

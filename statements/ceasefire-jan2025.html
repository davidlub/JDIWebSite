JDI Statement on the Ceasefire

The Jewish Democratic Initiative welcomes the ceasefire agreement between Israel and Hamas as a necessary and long-overdue first step towards ending the devastating conflict in Gaza.

We call on all parties to honour the terms of the agreement in full — including the release of all remaining hostages and Palestinian prisoners, and the unimpeded delivery of humanitarian aid to Gaza's civilian population.

A ceasefire is not a peace. JDI calls for this moment to be used to build towards a permanent end to the conflict, a political horizon that ensures security, dignity and self-determination for both Israelis and Palestinians, and an end to the occupation of Palestinian territory.

We mourn every life lost in this conflict — Israeli, Palestinian, and all others caught in the violence. We renew our commitment to a just and lasting peace.

Statement by the Jewish Democratic Initiative, 16 January 2025.

The Jewish Democratic Initiative represents a group of South African Jews who believe in social justice, pluralism, and a fairly negotiated resolution to the Israeli-Palestinian conflict, involving all parties and inspired by South Africa's own negotiated settlement.

Our Core Values

What We Stand For

Guided by the Jewish tradition of tikkun olam — repairing the world — we advocate for a South Africa and a world rooted in justice.

⚖️

Social Justice

We stand with all South Africans in the struggle against poverty, inequality, and systemic discrimination. Justice is not divisible.

🕊️

Peace & Non-Violence

We support a negotiated resolution to the Israeli-Palestinian conflict — ending the occupation and ensuring security, equal rights and dignity for all.

🌍

Democracy & Human Rights

We believe in universal human rights, international law, and the right of all peoples to self-determination and freedom from oppression.

🤝

Pluralism & Inclusion

We champion a South African Jewish community that is diverse, open, and tolerant — welcoming of different political views and backgrounds.

📢

Independent Voice

We speak independently of institutional communal bodies, providing a space for Jews who feel unrepresented by mainstream organisations.

✡️

Jewish Identity

Our values are rooted in Jewish tradition — in the prophetic call for justice, compassion for the stranger, and responsibility for one another.

Why JDI Needs Your Support

JDI is entirely independent — we receive no funding from any government, embassy, or institutional body. We are funded solely by members and supporters who believe in our mission.

This independence is our greatest asset. It means we can say what needs to be said, without fear of losing funding or facing political pressure.

But independence has a cost. Your donation directly funds our statements, events, campaigns, and communications. Without your support, we cannot continue.

"JDI is the only organisation in South Africa saying what so many South African Jews think but feel they cannot say publicly." — JDI Supporter

Festival of Dangerous Ideas 2026

Programme

Our annual weekend festival presented simultaneously at venues in Cape Town and Johannesburg. Saturday 18 July & Sunday 19 July.

The JDI's second Festival of Dangerous Ideas brings together the voices you won't hear at your shul, your dinner table, or your corporate gala. Over two extraordinary days, Saturday 18 and Sunday 19 July, we host the conversations that matter most, simultaneously in Cape Town and Johannesburg.

We have invited sought-after international and local speakers. The underlying theme is: how do Jews and Palestinians break free of the spiral of violence and find peace? We have chosen a symbolic date, Nelson Mandela's birthday, to emphasise that even under the grimmest circumstances, peace is still possible when there is a determination to find it.

The Festival takes place in two venues, The Hellenic Community Centre in Mouille Point, Cape Town, and the Killarney Country Club in Houghton, Johannesburg. Most sessions are screened live between the two venues, but some are local. A lunch, a supper and teas and coffee are included. There is plenty of secure parking at both venues. Check out the programme below and the speakers, then book on Quicket.

Membership

Become a Member

JDI membership is free and open to any South African Jew who shares our commitment to justice, democracy, and peace.

As a member you will:

  • ✦ Receive our newsletter and all JDI communications
  • ✦ Be invited to members-only events and discussions
  • ✦ Have your voice heard on the issues JDI takes positions on
  • ✦ Vote at the Annual General Meeting
  • ✦ Join a network of like-minded South African Jews
Membership does not require agreement with every statement JDI issues — only support for the mission of an independent, progressive Jewish voice.

Partner Organisations

JDI works in solidarity with progressive Jewish, interfaith, and human rights organisations in South Africa and internationally.

South Africa

South African Partners

Organisations within South Africa who share our commitment to social justice, human rights, and peace.

✊

South African BDS Coalition

The South African chapter of the global Boycott, Divestment and Sanctions movement, advocating for Palestinian rights through economic and cultural pressure.

Civil Society
⚖️

Helen Suzman Foundation

A South African think-tank dedicated to liberal democracy, human rights, and the rule of law — named after the legendary Jewish MP.

Democracy
🕌

Muslim Judicial Council

We engage in regular dialogue and coalition work with the MJC on shared concerns around Palestinian rights and interfaith solidarity in South Africa.

Interfaith
📚

Foundation for Human Rights

A South African organisation promoting access to justice, constitutional rights, and support for marginalised communities.

Human Rights
🌿

SECTION27

A South African public interest law centre focused on health, basic education, and social security — areas where Jewish values demand engagement.

Human Rights
🤲

South African Council of Churches

We work alongside faith communities across South Africa in joint advocacy on social justice, poverty, and peacebuilding.

Interfaith
International

International Jewish Progressive Partners

Jewish organisations around the world who share our vision of peace, justice, and an end to occupation.

✡️

Jewish Voice for Peace (USA)

A leading American Jewish organisation opposing the occupation, settlement expansion, and US military aid to Israel. One of the largest progressive Jewish movements globally.

USA
✡️

B'Tselem

The Israeli human rights organisation documenting rights violations in the occupied territories. One of the most authoritative sources on occupation practices.

Israel
✡️

Na'amod (UK)

British Jews standing against the occupation — a grassroots organisation that has brought thousands of UK Jews into the movement for Palestinian rights.

UK
✡️

If Not Now (USA)

A movement of American Jews working to end US support for the occupation and transform the Jewish community's relationship with Israel.

USA
✡️

Yesh Din

An Israeli volunteer human rights organisation monitoring and documenting Israeli law violations against Palestinians in the West Bank.

Israel
✡️

Independent Jewish Voices (Canada)

A Canadian organisation of Jewish progressives advocating for Palestinian rights and a just peace, independent of communal establishment bodies.

Canada

Humanitarian & Aid Organisations

Organisations providing humanitarian assistance in Gaza, the West Bank, and to refugees — that JDI recommends for donations.

🏥

Médecins Sans Frontières (MSF)

MSF provides emergency medical care to those affected by conflict, disease, and disaster. They have a significant presence in Gaza.

Medical
🌊

UNRWA

The United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestine Refugees — the primary provider of education, healthcare, and food assistance to Palestinian refugees.

UN Agency
🍞

World Central Kitchen

A chef-led humanitarian organisation providing meals in response to humanitarian crises, with significant operations in Gaza.

Food Aid
